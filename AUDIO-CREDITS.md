# Piano sample attribution

FluidR3_GM acoustic grand piano soundfont by Frank Wen and contributors.
Pre-rendered MP3 distribution: gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts.

Source: https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts
Sample bank: https://gleitz.github.io/midi-js-soundfonts/FluidR3_GM/acoustic_grand_piano-mp3.js
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

This project extracts the C4, D4, E4, F4, G4, A4 and B4 samples without modifying the audio data, bundles them in piano-samples.js, and applies playback gain and note-release envelopes at runtime.
